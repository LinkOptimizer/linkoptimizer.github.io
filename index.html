How It Works

Step 1: Select and Analyze

Open your InDesign document. LinkOptimizer scans all embedded links, identifying optimization opportunities based on your settings.

Step 2: Configure Workflow

Set resolution targets, format conversions, color adjustments, and sharpening. Apply Photoshop actions or layer-specific rules as needed.

Step 3: Process and Reimport

LinkOptimizer handles the heavy lifting in Photoshop, then seamlessly reimports optimized links into InDesign at full quality.

Step 4: Review and Export

Verify results in real-time. Batch multiple files for accelerated output, ready for print or web.
